Our Story

More Than a Café,
It's a Vibe

Cafe Moishi was born from a simple idea — to create a space where great food, premium shisha and unforgettable moments come together. Our lounge is designed for those who appreciate the finer things in life, offering a unique blend of comfort, luxury and good energy.

The Journey

Crafted forGood Times

What started as a passion for great flavours and beautiful spaces has grown into a destination loved by many. At Cafe Moishi, we bring people together — over exquisite food, signature shisha and an atmosphere that feels like home.

Every detail, from our interior to our menu, is carefully chosen to give you an experience that's not just enjoyable, but memorable.
