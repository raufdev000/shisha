About Cafe Moishi

Where the city exhales,
and the night begins to glow.

Set above the rooftops of Rome, Cafe Moishi is a quiet departure from the street below — a terrace built for slow evenings, where the skyline turns amber at dusk and the cocktail list is written to match the hour.
